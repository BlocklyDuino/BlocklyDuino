Connection & display settings

Monitor and Plotter share one connection. Changing port, baud or disconnecting affects both; pausing this view does not.

Send uses UTF-8. Arduino receives with Serial.available() and Serial.read(). The view retains up to 200,000 characters / 5,000 records. Saving exports the retained records, independent of the filter.
