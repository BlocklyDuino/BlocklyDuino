Named areas in your program

Use areas to explain the parts of a larger workspace: inputs, interrupt handlers, display updates, motor control, or a state machine. Each area has a name, a description, a color, and a rectangle.

Add an area

  1. Click Areas in the workspace toolbar, then + Add area.
  2. Drag a rectangle around the relevant blocks. You can start on a block or on empty workspace.
  3. Enter a name, an optional description and a color. Click Add area.
  4. Click Done with areas to return to normal block editing.

Escape cancels a rectangle or a move in progress. Press it again to leave area editing. Areas and Select stacks are separate editing modes; selecting one turns the other off.

Edit and navigate

  • Click Areas, then select a name in Choose an area…. The view centers on that area without changing your current zoom. Click Show to center and zoom to fit the selected area: a quick way to jump from a distant overview back to the part you want to work on.
  • Click Edit or double-click the area to change its name, description and color.
  • Drag its colored title strip to move the rectangle. Select an area, then drag its bottom-right handle to resize it.
  • Delete area removes the annotation. Undo area undoes the last area change (up to 20 changes in the current project session).
  • Mouse-wheel zoom keeps the point under your cursor in place. Areas stay aligned with their workspace positions at every zoom level.
Areas are annotations. Moving or deleting an area does not move or delete any blocks. Use Select stacks to rearrange the program itself. Areas do not affect generated Arduino code, memory usage, timing, or hardware.

Save, reopen and start over

Areas are saved inside your normal project XML alongside the blocks. Autosave, opening a saved project and recovery copies retain them. New project clears both blocks and areas after keeping the recovery copy. Older projects without areas open normally.

Areas live in this local BlocklyDuino editor. An unrelated editor may ignore or discard their extra XML metadata. Keep your original XML when sharing a project with another application.
